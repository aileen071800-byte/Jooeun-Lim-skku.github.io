01 — About Me

A dancer
who reads
the city.

Hello! I'm Jooeun Lim, a 4th-year Dance major at Sungkyunkwan University. I chose my research theme from a deep personal love for pop-up events — those fleeting, immersive experiences that transform a neighborhood into something alive and electric.

I'm especially drawn to Seongsu-dong and Hannam-dong, two of Seoul's most vibrant cultural districts. I visit pop-ups regularly, observing how MZ generation consumers move through space, form emotional connections with brands, and claim culture as their own.
