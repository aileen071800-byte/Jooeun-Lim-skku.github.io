01 — Who I Am

About Me

Hello! I'm Jooeun Lim, a 4th-year Dance major at Sungkyunkwan University. I chose my research theme because of my deep love for pop-up events — those fleeting, immersive experiences that transform a neighborhood into something alive.

I'm especially drawn to Seongsu-dong and Hannam-dong, two of Seoul's most vibrant cultural districts, where I visit pop-ups regularly and observe how generation Z and millennials consume space, art, and brand experiences in new ways.
