01

Seongsu-dong

Seoul's #1 Pop-up District · 성수동

A former industrial zone of repurposed factories, Seongsu hosts more pop-ups than any other neighbourhood in Korea. The East Yeonmujang-gil strip is the current hotspot, with 2026 seeing Pokémon Mega Festa, BLACKPINK DEADLINE, and Musinsa's new megastore all launching here.

02

Hannam-dong

Premium Lifestyle Belt · 한남동

Seoul's gallery and boutique corridor. Hannam attracts luxury, niche fragrance, and art-forward pop-ups. The Hangang-jin to Hannam Crossroads stretch is lined with curated independent spaces favoured by design-conscious consumers.

03

Hongdae

University Culture · 홍대

University culture meets indie creativity. Hongdae is the epicentre for K-pop fan pop-ups, beauty events, and independent artist markets. AK Plaza Hongdae and Olive Young Hongdae Town are key anchor venues in 2025–2026.

04

Gangnam · The Hyundai

Retail Power Zone · 강남 · 여의도

The Hyundai Seoul in Yeouido rivals Seongsu as Korea's top pop-up venue. The K League × Sanrio collab here drew 250,000 visitors in 2024. Department stores and malls drive high-volume sales-focused pop-ups across all categories.

05

Other Areas

Jamsil · Yongsan · Myeongdong · DDP

Pop-up culture has spread across all of Seoul. Department stores, outlet malls, and campus areas now serve as key pop-up venues, reflecting the democratisation of the format beyond its original Seongsu epicentre.

Research Insight

Key Trends 2024–2026

Field research by Jooeun Lim combined with data from Popga (1,431 pop-ups) and Seongsu Gorilla platform analytics.

21%
IP & Character #1
21% of all 2024 pop-ups were IP/character. K League × Sanrio hit 250,000 visitors. In 2026, Pokémon Mega Festa spans 3 simultaneous Seongsu events.
11%
Beauty Boom
160 beauty pop-ups in 2024. By 2026, luxury brands like YSL and La Roche-Posay run dedicated immersive experiences — not just product stalls.
32%
East Yeonmujang Surge
32% of Seongsu pop-ups concentrated on East Yeonmujang-gil in H1 2025. Brands need larger raw spaces for deeper content design.
52%
Merch T-Shirt Rise
T-shirt mentions in pop-up communities rose 52% YoY. Graphic tees have become identity markers for Gen Z — not just souvenirs.
↑
Pop-up Town Format
Multi-brand "pop-up towns" — Musinsa Festa, Coupang Beauty Show — maximise cost efficiency and footfall. The format accelerates into 2026.
NEW
Seoul First Launches
In 2026, global tours (BLACKPINK DEADLINE, Pokémon 30th) now launch in Seoul before other world markets — confirming Seoul's global pop-up leadership.
